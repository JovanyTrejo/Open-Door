Coyote Check-In
A student wellness web app that gives students one place to check in on how they're doing, book time with wellness staff, and get help for a friend they're worried about. Crisis resources are available from every page.
Built as a team project for CSE 6550 Software Engineering at California State University, San Bernardino (CSUSB).
> **Not an emergency service.** If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call **911** or campus police. For mental health crisis support, call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
---
Features
Crisis access – Links to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline and campus emergency contacts on every page.
Login – Registered users sign in to personal accounts. Students and wellness staff see different pages based on their role.
Wellness check-in – Students record their name, the date, their overall mood, and anything else on their mind, then can jump straight to booking an appointment or reaching the hotline.
Appointment booking – A calendar shows available dates and which staff are free at a selected time. Students give a reason for the visit and submit a request.
Walk-in requests – A Walk-In button for students who want same-day help.
Report someone – Students, faculty, or staff can report concern about another person. The page shows guidance on how to help in the meantime and a warning against misuse. Reports are visible only to authorized wellness staff.
Wellness info – Self-care advice, About Us/FAQ, and contact pages.
